# Author Contribution

I conceived and developed this project as a geospatial planning investigation of the mismatch between urban green-infrastructure capacity and ecosystem-service demand across Tartu.

My contribution includes problem formulation, analytical design, geospatial implementation, integration of remote-sensing, land-cover and population data, development of the capacity-demand-deficit framework, sensitivity testing, spatial interpretation, cartographic communication and planning recommendations.

The analysis was used to identify where green-infrastructure protection, enhancement, restoration or nature-based intervention may provide the greatest planning value. Recommended responses include street-tree expansion, pocket parks, courtyard greening, green roofs and walls, permeable surfaces, stormwater-oriented green infrastructure and improved pedestrian connections to existing green assets.

External datasets and established geospatial methods remain attributable to their original providers and methodological sources.
