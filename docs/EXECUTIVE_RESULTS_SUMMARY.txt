EXECUTIVE RESULTS SUMMARY

The validated compact urban study area covers 38.888 km².
Built-up land is the dominant mapped surface, occupying 21.426 km²,
equivalent to 55.10% of the study area. Vegetated urban green
infrastructure—including dense urban forest, urban woodland, healthy green space
and stressed green space—covers 16.619 km² or
42.74% of the area.

The ecosystem-service planning-priority assessment identifies
20.230 km², or 52.02% of the study
area, as High or Very High priority. Very High priority zones alone occupy
12.092 km², representing 31.09% of the
mapped area.

The spatial concentration of urgent planning need is strongly associated with
built-up surfaces. Approximately 19.955 km² of
built-up land falls within High or Very High priority zones. This represents
93.14% of all mapped built-up land and
98.64% of the total High and Very High priority area.

Mean ecosystem-service deficit increases systematically from
0.104 in Very Low priority areas to
0.858 in Very High priority areas. The Very High priority
mean is approximately 8.3 times the mean recorded in
Very Low priority areas, demonstrating a clear and internally consistent planning
gradient.
