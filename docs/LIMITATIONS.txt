LIMITATIONS

1. The UGI classification is a planning-oriented representation derived from remote sensing and land-cover products, not a field-validated habitat inventory.

2. WorldPop 2020 is used as a population-based demand proxy. Alignment to a 10 m reporting grid does not imply native 10 m census observations.

3. Ecosystem-service capacity is a relative index rather than a direct measurement of individual services such as cooling, carbon sequestration, recreation or runoff retention.

4. Socioeconomic vulnerability, age, public access, ownership, cost and intervention feasibility are not directly included in the deficit model.

5. The five priority classes are relative to conditions within Tartu and should be recalibrated before application elsewhere.

6. Site-specific interventions require field investigation and local planning assessment.

7. A small raster fringe occurs along the vector boundary because of grid discretisation. Final reporting uses a centre-cell boundary mask covering 38.8872 km², closely matching the 38.887971 km² vector study area.
