## Capacity, demand and deficit

Mean ecosystem-service capacity is **41.54/100**, mean demand is **0.5768**, and mean deficit is **0.4110**.

Built-up areas show the strongest capacity-demand mismatch, with mean capacity **8.00/100**, mean demand **0.7339**, and mean deficit **0.6751**.

Dense Urban Forest has mean capacity **98.82/100** and mean deficit **0.0031**, while Urban Woodland has mean capacity **77.81/100** and mean deficit **0.0597**.

## Planning priority

High priority covers **7.8075 km²** and Very High priority covers **7.8154 km²**. Together they account for approximately **15.6229 km² (40.17%)** of the reporting area.

The strongest planning result is the concentration of priority within built-up land: **72.30% of built-up land** falls within High or Very High priority classes, compared with only **0.02% of non-built land**.

Deficit and planning priority have a Spearman correlation of **0.9802**, consistent with priority being derived from the deficit ranking.
