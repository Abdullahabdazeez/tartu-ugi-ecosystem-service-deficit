# Reproducibility

The repository provides the final boundary-masked reporting rasters, summary tables, methodology and final maps used to communicate the study results.

Core specifications:

- CRS: **EPSG:3301**
- Reporting resolution: **10 m**
- Reporting area: **38.8872 km²**
- Deficit formula: **Demand × (1 − Capacity/100)**
- Demand weighting: **85% population + 15% built context**
- Planning-priority classification: **five deficit quintiles**

The processed rasters and tables in `data/processed/` provide the final numerical and spatial products used in the report and figures.
