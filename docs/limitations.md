# Limitations

- The UGI classification is a planning-oriented representation derived from remote sensing and land-cover products, not a field-validated habitat inventory.
- WorldPop 2020 is used as a population-based demand proxy. Alignment to a 10 m reporting grid does not imply native 10 m census observations.
- Ecosystem-service capacity is a relative index rather than a direct measurement of individual services such as cooling, carbon sequestration, recreation or runoff retention.
- Socioeconomic vulnerability, age, public access, ownership, cost and intervention feasibility are not directly included in the deficit model.
- The five priority classes are relative to conditions within Tartu and should be recalibrated before application elsewhere.
- Site-specific interventions require field investigation and local planning assessment.
