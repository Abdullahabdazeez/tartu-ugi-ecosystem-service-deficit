## First prepared release

This repository presents an integrated urban green-infrastructure and ecosystem-service deficit assessment for Tartu, Estonia.

### Included

- Urban green-infrastructure classification
- Ecosystem-service capacity mapping
- Ecosystem-service demand assessment
- Capacity–demand deficit modelling
- Five-class planning-priority analysis
- UGI composition by planning-priority class
- Planning-action register
- Five final maps and six analytical charts
- Final production notebook
- Technical report, methodology and limitations
- Result-reproduction and repository-validation scripts
- Automated GitHub Actions validation

### Principal findings

- Built-up land covered **55.10%** of the 38.89 km² compact urban study area.
- Vegetated UGI covered **42.74%**.
- Tree-based UGI covered **25.32%**.
- High and Very High planning priority covered **52.02%**, approximately **20.23 km²**.
- Very High priority alone covered **31.09%**, approximately **12.09 km²**.
- Approximately **19.96 km²** of built-up land occurred within High or Very High priority zones.
- Mean ecosystem-service capacity was **36.71**.
- Mean ecosystem-service demand was **0.3787**.
- Mean ecosystem-service deficit was **0.5135**.

### Interpretation

The priority surface identifies areas where green-infrastructure enhancement and ecosystem-service investment may produce the greatest planning benefit. It is intended for strategic screening and should be combined with local site analysis, land-ownership information, infrastructure constraints and community needs.
